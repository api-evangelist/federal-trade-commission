# Federal Trade Commission

> The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) is the U.S. federal agency that enforces antitrust
> and consumer protection law. It operates one small public, read-only REST API at
> https://api.ftc.gov/v0 covering Do Not Call complaint reports and Hart-Scott-Rodino
> early termination notices, plus a Project Open Data (DCAT-US 1.1) inventory of 95
> datasets at https://www.ftc.gov/data.json. Its two other developer-facing programmes —
> the National Do Not Call Registry telemarketer download and the Consumer Sentinel
> Network — are gated behind registration and law-enforcement vetting and publish no
> public contract.

Generated 2026-09-09 by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from this repository's
artifacts and the FTC's own public documentation. The FTC does not serve an llms.txt of

## Getting started

- Base URL: https://api.ftc.gov/v0
- Authentication: an api.data.gov API key, sent as `?api_key=` or the `X-Api-Key` header
- Free key signup: https://api.data.gov/signup/
- Try it with no signup: append `?api_key=DEMO_KEY` (rate limited per IP address)
- HTTPS is required; plaintext requests are rejected with HTTP 400
- Responses are JSON / JSON:API 1.0; pages are capped at 50 records

## Operations

- GET /v0/dnc-complaints — search consumer reports of unwanted and robocall telemarketing.
  Filters: created_date[_from|_to], violation_date[_from|_to], state, city (requires
  state), area_code, is_robocall, sort_order, items_per_page (max 50), offset.
- GET /v0/dnc-complaints/{id} — one complaint by its 32-character hex id.
- GET /v0/hsr-early-termination-notices — search premerger early termination notices.
  Filters: title (CONTAINS supported), transaction_number, date, sort_by (created|changed),
  sort_order, items_per_page, offset. The programme was suspended in February 2021; the
  endpoint serves the historical record.

There are no write operations. Nothing in this API creates, changes or deletes anything.

## Gated programmes (no public contract)

- National Do Not Call Registry, telemarketer access: https://telemarketing.donotcall.gov
  Registration and a statutory annual per-area-code fee are required.
- Consumer Sentinel Network: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/consumer-sentinel-network
  Restricted to vetted law enforcement.
- HSR premerger e-filing: https://www.hsr.gov

## What the FTC does not publish

Recorded so an agent does not waste calls looking: no OpenAPI, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, gRPC or
WSDL description; no webhooks or event stream; no MCP server; no A2A agent card; no
/.well-known documents of any kind (security.txt, api-catalog, openid-configuration all
404 or soft-404 as of 2026-09-09); no SDKs or CLI in any package registry; no changelog,
status page, SLA or deprecation policy; no pricing, because access is free.
